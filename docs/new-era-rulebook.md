## Complete Rules
## 1. Terms and Conditions

This document serves as a contractual agreement between all parties involved in the New Era Fantasy Football Leagues. This document serves as a guideline, rulebook, and operations manual for the New Era Fantasy Football Leagues.

When you accept the invitation to join a New Era league, send in your buy-in, and/or attend the draft, it is assumed that you read, understand, and agree with all of the rules, guidelines, penalties, and regulations detailed in this rulebook.

It is also assumed that you thoroughly read and agree with the terms and conditions in this section.

When managers send in their buy-in, it is assumed that they read and agree with **section 3.** They understand that New Era Fantasy Football Leagues do not offer refunds on deposits, buy-ins, or in the case of NFIF, future buy-ins. Please reference **section 3.** It applies to all cases, inside or outside of this rulebook, whether mentioned or not.

It is the responsibility of league members to review the rulebook annually. The rulebook is split into sections. When reviewing the rule book league members should:

- Reference **<u>Latest updates</u>** section

- Review of "**Rules for New Era Fantasy League's"** section

- Review of section for league(s) member is participating in.

The rulebook cannot be drastically altered mid-contest year (unless exception noted) but will likely be updated every year. Updates will be notated above for quick reference, but we are human, and mistakes happen. When sending your buy-in and participating in a draft, it is assumed that you responsibly review the rule book annually at your own discretion.

While league members are not required to have Instagram, it is strongly recommended. The league is authorized to run events exclusively through Instagram (**Section 11c**). Adding that element into the league adds an extra bit of excitement to getting a little surprise while scrolling on your social media feed. Again, you **<u>are not required to have an Instagram</u>**, *but not having one means you understand and accept that there may be events run exclusively through channels like Instagram that you either might not qualify for, or may not be aware of.* Of course, major news would come in the announcements chat on WhatsApp

League members are not required to be in the group chat, but it is extremely recommended. All major news is discussed or announced in the group chat. To qualify for league-wide events, you must be in the group chat. If you are not in the group chat, and there is an ongoing vote, you forfeit your vote, if you are not in the chat. Again, you **<u>are not required to be in the group chat</u>**, *but by you not being in the group chat, you understand and accept any of the decisions made in your absence, and you accept that your place in the league may be in jeopardy if your absence from the chat is for a long duration.*

All league members understand that if they express any sentiment that they no longer want to be in the league, or don't plan on staying (**section 9b**), then processes to replace or expel disgruntled manager will begin (**section 9g**), as with all cases in the New Era Fantasy Football League’s, managers will not qualify for a refund (**section 3).**

When managers send in their buy-in, it is assumed that they read and agree with **section 9.** Violations of **section 9** could result in immediate expulsion and ban of involved managers.

When managers send in their buy-in, it is assumed that they read and agree with **section 8b.** One violation of **section 8b** *<u>will result in immediate expulsion and ban</u>* of involved managers.

<u>For commissioners and Game Wardens:</u> When accepting the role and responsibility of commissioner you swear to Allah that you will try to run a fair contest to the best of your ability, even if it is at the expense of your own team. You are expected to review the rulebook annually, consult with the rulebook when necessary, and be the heart of your respective league. You’re expected to be in the group chat, and the group chat is expected to be unmuted. Failure to do so could result in the implementation of **sections 4j. or 4k.** depending on seriousness.

Commissioners also swear to Allah that if **sections 4l., 4j., or 4k.** are implemented, they will honor and follow through with these processes, and not do anything to disturb the processes, or influence the outcome, outside of ways advised in these guidelines.

Commissioners swear to Allah that if these processes result with the removal of their leadership responsibilities, and/or impeachment, it will happen in a civil and peaceful manner. Commissioners swear to Allah that if this were to happen, they will stay in the league as a member and forfeit digital league ownership to the New Era Game Warden, or to proper authority depending on situation.

### 4f. Voting
>
> The New Era Fantasy Football League's do not vote on matters often, and that's a good thing. However, during times when matters are brought to vote in any New Era League, the active commissioner does not get a vote.
>
> With that being said, the active commissioner of a New Era Fantasy League does get a tie-breaker vote, and the right to veto.
>
> The co-commissioner does get a vote, but they don't have veto powers.
>
### 4g. Accepting Trades
>
> In New Era Fantasy Football leagues, it is advised that both commissioners communicate prior to accepting a trade, to ensure there isn't any foul play. If they do not communicate, and one or the other accepts a pending trade, it is not a big deal.
>
> However, *<u>commissioners are NOT allowed to accept their own trades.</u>* When a commissioner is involved in a trade, the commissioner *who is not involved* is responsible for vetting the trade, ensuring no foul play, and ultimately accepting the trade. In this instance, the commissioner not involved in the trade, is the lone commissioner investigating it. **(Section 4m.)**
>
> The *first violation* of this rule will result in a *verbal warning.* Speaking from experience, this type of thing accidentally happens when coming into the season after a long off-season. Even if it's week 4, if there aren't a lot of trades until that point, the commissioner is still rusty, so cut them some slack.
>
> After the first violation is followed by verbal warning, we will move on to a 3-strike system. *If a commissioner accepts their own trade 4 times in the same season*, then that commissioner will be suspended for the remainder of the season (**section 4j**).
>
> The suspended commissioner will reassume their role at the start of the next season, however, *if they accept their own trade two times in the same season following suspension,* their position will be immediately vacant (**section 4k.**), and elections will begin to fill the vacancy for the upcoming season (**section 4l.**).
>
### 4h. Cheating
>
> If any (or both) of the commissioners are caught in violation of guidelines in this rule book, blatantly cheating, misusing/abusing commissioner powers, or in rare cases going on a power trip, then **sections 4j, 4k, and 4l** could be explored and implemented depending on degree of violation.
>
> These conditions are defined as follows:
>
> *Blatantly cheating* - Obvious cheating that may or may not be identified in this rule book, trade collusion (**section 8b.**), or editing your own or another team’s roster after gametime.
>
> *Misusing/Abusing Commissioner Powers* - Exploiting the commissioner powers in any way to give your own team a competitive advantage. The rule of thumb here is if team X doesn't have the capability to do something, you shouldn't either. This applies to editing bench and extending waiver dates for personal advantage, amongst other things.
>
> *Power Trip* - Trying to implement **section 7** without a reason, or just acting absolutely crazy.
>
> *"Violations of this rule book" -* The rulebook isn't perfect, and not everything is covered. The expectation is that this rulebook is resorted to first by commissioners when making a decision, and that may not happen all of the time which is fine, unless it affects the fairness of any contest in any capacity. Chronic disregard, negligence, and disrespect to this rulebook and guidelines listed would reflect the context of this phrase.
>
### 4i. Term Limits
>
> New Era Fantasy Football League's do not have term limits.
>
> I do not expect any changes in leadership, but in the extremely rare instance where a commissioner in a particular league is impeached mid-year, the commissioner still in office will assume complete commissioner responsibilities, and/or, GW may assume vacated responsibilities.
>
> **Sections 4j-4m** cover these topics thoroughly.
>
### 4j. Suspension
>
> Minor violations of **sections 1 or 4m** result in a suspension.
>
> Four violations of **section 4g.** within the same season result in a suspension.
>
> The severity of violation is to be determined from guidelines in **4m.**
>
> Commissioner is to be considered temporarily suspended during process of **4m.**
>
> Suspensions typically are season long but could be given shorter or longer lengths depending on the outcome of **4m.**
>
> Game Warden suspensions are handled in the same manner, but with a vote between the 3 commissioners and Moonie Mustafa. Unanimous vote results in suspension, with its length determined by party making judgement.
>
### 4k. Impeachment
>
> If found at fault through **4m**:
>
> Violations of **section 8b and/or section 1** could result in impeachment.  
> Six violations of **section 4g.** across two seasons result in impeachment.
>
> Impeachment would be determined through the outcome of **4m.**
>
> If one commissioner is impeached, New Era Game Warden is encouraged to get involved.
>
> The options at that point will be:

1.  Lone standing commissioner runs the league alone

2.  New Era GW assumes commissioner responsibilities

3.  An interim commissioner is appointed, and elections begin.

> That decision would be made during the process of **4m.** upon discovery of at-fault verdict.
>
> Campaigns would begin upon impeachment, and elections would occur per **section 4l** guidelines.
>
> ***Game Warden impeachments*** are handled in the same manner, but with a vote between the 3 commissioners and Moonie Mustafa.
>
> Unanimous vote results in impeachment.
>
> Moonie Mustafa would assume Game Warden responsibilities in wake of Big Blue impeachment.
>
> *If he declines the responsibility,* he is to be forced to assume it in any capacity until new GW is appointed, but the 3 New Era Commissioner’s would together take on most of the role in this instance.
>
### 4l. Elections
>
> Very rarely will elections occur in New Era Fantasy Football League's. Being candid, the league is structured in a way where it would be difficult to remove someone from leadership, without them outright cheating.
>
> Nonetheless, in these guidelines we have mapped out the factors that would need to occur for this section to be implemented.
>
> If this section were to be implemented, the following sequence of events should be followed through with:

1.  Commissioner suspected of violating a particular or multiple codes.

2.  Violation is investigated as instructed in **4m.**

3.  Commissioner is found in clear violation of a particular or multiple codes.

4.  New Era Leadership (not in question) within the same league, or across leagues depending on circumstance, meet and decide whether or not election is declared.

> ***\*Note: This process does not involve the commissioners voting on whether or not an election will occur. They don’t even have to agree with each other. I just don't want the decision to be rushed\*\****

5.  The commissioner in charge of that specific league, or whoever is left in charge of it per guidelines, would declare an election.  
      
    *\*\*An official election could only occur if declared by commissioning power.*  
      
    *\*\*\*If an election is declared, it does not mean that the penalized commissioner is impeached (**4k**). That would depend on severity of violation and outcome of **4m.  
      
    \*\***\*\*Requirement to qualify for election is 10 years in The New Era Fantasy Football Leagues (or reasonably less but within that range)*

6.  League members would campaign against incumbent commissioner, who also has an opportunity to campaign, appeal to the league, and retain role through election, if not impeached as a result of **4m**.

7.  The campaign would begin at the point that elections are declared, and the actual election would occur during championship week.  
      
    (If the incident is in off-season or whatever, it must be before a milestone point. i.e. Championship, start of season, start of playoffs \[preferred\])

8.  Upon victory, the newly elected commissioner will begin duty the next season. If an incumbent wins, business as usual.

> Again, **<u>we don't expect this section to ever be used</u>**, but this is part of the way we have proper checks and balances, to ensure a fair contest for all, and maintain the high standard of New Era Fantasy Football Leagues.
>
> If anything else comes up during this process that was not anticipated in these guidelines, see **section 5.**
>
### 4m. Investigations
>
> Similar to **section 4g.,** when a commissioner is involved in a trade, the commissioner *who is not involved, is the only commissioner vetting, investigating, and ultimately accepting a trade.*
>
> This is to be <u>done without interference from the counterpart commissioner</u>. We want to encourage everyone to take their roles seriously, so you cannot be offended when you are being questioned by someone view yourself above or equal to in hierarchy.
>
> If there is no counterpart commissioner, this is to be handled by New Era Game Warden. If New Era GW does not qualify to be involved for whatever reason, Moonie Mustafa would serve as his backfill **section 4b.**
>
> When a **commissioner is under investigation for a code violation**, the same rules apply, but with stricter boundaries. The primary commissioner involved in the investigation, is the one not under investigation. The commissioner under investigation is not allowed to get involved or interfere, or else they risk immediate and mandatory suspension **(section 4j)**.
>
> When a commissioner is under investigation, the Game Warden is encouraged to get involved. If the Game Warden is the one under investigation, Moonie Mustafa and all New Era commissioners are encouraged to get involved.
>
> If Game Warden and a New Era commissioner are under investigation, then commissioners not in question, along with Moonie Mustafa, investigate and resolve the matter **(section 4b.).**
>
### 4n. Rulebook review
>
> Commissioners are required to review the rulebook and all areas that concern them at the start of every season. This should be done as a team.

I have a safe, and we could get into the intricates of all this, it doesn't have to be in writing, but the principle here is, you don't want it mixed with your money, and it shouldn’t be stored digitally.

## 9. The New Era Standard

Across all New Era Fantasy Football League's we hold our managers to standards in terms of competitiveness, compliance to rules and regulations, and league culture.

Violations of **section 9** could result in a manager’s exit from all New Era Fantasy Football League's that apply.

Severe or repeated **section 9** violations could result in expulsion and ban (**section 9g**)

### 9a. Performance
>
> Moving forward, all New Era League's expect managers to manage at least 1 playoff berth in 5 years.
>
> If a particular manager is not able to reach this threshold, and isn't showing significant improvement in the case of NFIF, then that particular managers team may be considered for sale without refund (**section 3**)
>
> This may or may not be implemented, and its on the leagues respective commissioner to track and act on.
>
> Obviously, WE DO NOT want to kick anyone out. It's hard enough finding good managers as it already is. This is just here to hold everyone to a standard. This league is going into 16th year, and we want everyone to be very competitive.
>
### 9b. The "I Quit" rule
>
> Unfortunately, over the years, we've seen a behavior where if a particular manager doesn't get what they want, they try to leverage their participation in the league, to get what they want.
>
> This is a behavior we do not tolerate in any of the leagues.
>
> As seen in the past, ***if any manager expresses the sentiment that they no longer want to be in the league,*** or don't plan on staying, if they don't get what they want, they are out without refund (**section 3**)
>
> Obviously, league commissioners would be the ones implementing this, and leagues will have their own standards in place (specifically a complex one for NFIF), but bottom line in general, we don't play that shit in The New Era.
>
> At the end of the day just remember, we are doing our best to give you the fairest experience of the most unfair game in the world! It's so frustrating. So much so, that things you don't mean could be said in the heat of the moment, and that's OK, but let's try to see big picture.
>
### 9c. Giving away players/Trying to fit in
>
> I really don't want to have to write this section, but we've encountered times in the past where we'd have a manager who for whatever reason would just start giving players away. It would be like a cousin or close friend calling for a player, and he'd bless them up.
>
> Looking across all formats at moment of writing (11/21/24) I don’t see any managers here who would do such a thing, but if we encounter this type of manager they need to be removed in the quickest and smoothest way possible.
>
### 9d. Dropping players
>
> Another toxic behavior that we haven’t experienced in probably a decade is a manager getting frustrated and dropping all their players.
>
> Historically, we dealt with this behavior harshly. We put all the players back on their bench, lock their team, and have them play the week and get a 0. This has cost people playoff berth; it's really harsh and probably the reason we haven't seen it in years humdillah.
>
> We're keeping that rule, and I'm adding that **9g** should be implemented.
>
> Lighter penalty suggestions for commissioners are either a heavy FAAB fine in NE2k, or an impactful but fair draft pick stripped in NFIF.
>
### 9e. Special Accommodations
>
> In general, and for the most part, New Era Fantasy League's do not offer special accommodations to league managers.
>
> Historically, we have been a progressive league that is usually ahead of the curve when it comes to trends or things like covid.
>
> Understandably, due to the competitive nature of managers, when it comes to accommodations, people tend prefer the competitive edge over competition.
>
> We had a manager this year in NE2k, with 11 injuries, and he had to start 14 players, with only a 9 man bench. It is what it is. Big Blue is usually in favor of some type of fix, but it's advised to rule against it unless extreme covid level anomaly.

### 35a. Contingent Trades (NFIF Protocol)

> Contingent trades are **permitted and enforceable** in NFIF, provided they adhere to the following guidelines:
>
> Like with FAAB trading (**10a**), immediately after completing a trade with a contingent agreement, both parties, or the party most at risk, **must make one of the commissioners aware, or message in the group chat.**
>
> ***If a contingent trade goes unmentioned by both trading parties***, and no one is made aware within 2 hours of accepting the trade, then the ***agreement will be null and void.***
>
> After the Commissioner/GW is made aware, they ***will post it in a chat designated only for recording contingent trade agreements***.
>
> Commissioner/GW will be the only ones with capability to message in that chat, and the chat log **will be treated as a contractual agreement**, and it *will be enforced.*

### 35b. Taxi squad stealing

> ***Any team can attempt to steal a player from another team’s Taxi Squad by offering a draft pick of the same round from one of the next two drafts in exchange.***

*When presented with the offer**, *****Managers will only have two options:  
**

1.  Activate the player from their Taxi Squad, protecting them and blocking the trade.

2.  Accept the draft pick and complete the trade.***  
    ***

> **RULES AND PROCESS  
> *Poacher’s Post***

- <u>As of 10/28/26, all taxi-stealing attempts must be conducted in Poacher’s Post inside the New Era app, which is the official Poacher’s Post. The app notifies the defending manager and the league when a poach is sent.</u>

- The initiating manager submits the offer through the app, which automatically records:

  - The exact time and terms of the offer (the app’s timestamp replaces screenshots)

  - The player being targeted

  - The round of the future draft pick offered

***Setting Player Values***

> Player value is a same-round draft pick from one of the next two drafts. Picks further out are worth less and cannot be used. If a player was drafted in the second round, you have to send a second round pick. If they were drafted in the 3rd, or were an FA acquisition, it has to be a 3rd round pick.
>
> **DECISION TIMEFRAME**

***Response Time:***

- Managers have 24 hours to respond to a poach attempt, counted from the moment the poach is sent in the app. There are no active hours; the clock runs around the clock. They either elevate the player from taxi, or accept the poach.

> ***Grace Period:***

- There is no grace period – **24 hours and 1 minute pass without a response, the poaching attempt is automatically processed and enforced.  
  **

> **<u>During the decision period, the defending manager cannot accept any other trade offers until the poaching attempt is resolved.</u>**
>
> **PENALTIES AND ENFORCEMENT**
>
> ***Failure to respond***
>
> If the defending manager *does not respond within the timeframe* (24 hours), the **poaching attempt is automatically processed**.

***Invalid Offers***

> *If the poaching manager submits an invalid offer* (e.g., offering a pick of lesser value):

- Attempt is void

- Poaching Manager loses \$10 FAAB to defending manager as a penalty for poor sportsmanship.

> ***Second Offense:***
>
> The poaching manager loses **\$50 FAAB** to the defending manager, even if the invalid offer is made to a different manager.
>
> ***Third Offense:***
>
> The poaching manager **forfeits the same draft pick** they attempted to offer, awarding it to the defending manager. This penalty applies regardless of whether it involves the same defending manager.

***Bad Faith Actions***

> If the *defending manager tries to bypass the poaching attempt* (e.g., accepting another trade during the decision period):

- Notify the Commissioner/GW or post the issue in Poacher’s Post immediately. The app will also flag any trade the defending manager makes during the decision period and notify the whole league.

- The attempted trade will be nullified, and the poaching trade will be processed and enforced.

- Additional penalties may be applied at the discretion of the commissioning teams.

> ***Documentation***
>
> The app records every poaching attempt, response and deadline automatically. Similar to FAAB trade rules (10a) and contingent trades (13d), league members are still expected to self-report and self-police any infraction the app misses.
>
> All poaching activity must be conducted in Poacher’s Post in the New Era app.

- The app’s record of the offer, showing its time and terms, serves as official documentation and is required for enforcing the trade.

- Once a poach is completed, commissioners process the move in Sleeper.
