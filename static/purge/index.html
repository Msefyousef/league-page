Why nobody can rig it

At draw time, every phone reads the same public random number from drand, the League of Entropy randomness beacon. Nobody can know it early or change it. Result = first 12 hex digits of that number, mod the ball count. Below the red count means Purge. Beacon round: fixed at draw time
